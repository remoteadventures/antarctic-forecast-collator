Each element is judged only where the model provides it; hover or tap a cell to see every value and what set the colour. Visibility comes from ECMWF IFS, GFS, UKMO and AMPS only (AMPS from falling snow, so blowing snow isn't included). Cloud base: AMPS gives its own; for the other models it's inferred from the forecast sounding (lowest level reaching ice saturation, ignoring the lowest ~100 m). ECMWF IFS uses its 0.25° profile. "≤" means saturated at the lowest usable level. Low-cloud cover is the fallback where no profile exists. Any precip (≥ 0.01 mm/h water equivalent) is red; nil means < 0.01 mm/h.
